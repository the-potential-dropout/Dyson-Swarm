# Dyson Swarm

A semi-serious open research and engineering project exploring the design, simulation, business logic, and civilization-scale implications of a Dyson Swarm.

The project starts simple: collect knowledge, organize engineering questions, build simulations, and turn Discord chaos into a structured research initiative.

## Main Areas

- Knowledge & Tech Research
- Engineering Design
- Business
- Simulations
- Media & Community
- Meta / Philosophy

## Current Stage

Early foundation.

The goal is not to pretend we are building a Dyson Swarm tomorrow. The goal is to build a structured public knowledge base, simulation playground, and long-term engineering imagination around what such a project would require.
