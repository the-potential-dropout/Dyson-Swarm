# Dyson Swarm Basics

A Dyson Swarm is a large collection of independent satellites orbiting a star.

Each unit collects or redirects energy.

Unlike a rigid Dyson Sphere, a swarm can theoretically be built gradually over time using many separate collectors.

## Why a Swarm?

A rigid Dyson Sphere is generally considered physically unrealistic because:

- the structure would experience enormous stresses
- orbital mechanics become unstable
- the material requirements are extreme
- construction would be nearly impossible

A swarm is more realistic because:

- it is modular
- units can fail independently
- deployment can happen gradually
- satellites can maintain separate orbits
- maintenance becomes easier

## Core Questions

- How much energy can be collected?
- What orbit should the collectors use?
- How is energy transmitted?
- What materials are required?
- How are collisions avoided?
- How are collectors manufactured?
- How is heat rejected?
