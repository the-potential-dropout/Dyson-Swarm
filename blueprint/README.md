# Blueprint

This folder integrates the whole Dyson Swarm concept into a serious deployment-style blueprint.

The blueprint connects:

- research
- engineering
- simulations
- manufacturing
- supply chain
- logistics
- business
- governance
- environmental impact
- investor case

## Purpose

The goal is to make the fictional system legible as a real systems-engineering plan.

Every section should distinguish between:

- known physics
- plausible engineering
- speculative assumptions
- unresolved open questions
