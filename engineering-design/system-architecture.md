# System Architecture

A Dyson Swarm is a system of systems.

## Major Systems

- collector satellites
- orbital control
- power conversion
- power transmission
- heat rejection
- communications
- autonomy and maintenance
- manufacturing
- launch and logistics
- governance and safety
